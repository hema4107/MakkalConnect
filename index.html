MakkalConnect

MakkalConnect is a digital public service platform designed to connect citizens directly with government representatives and simplify the process of raising and tracking public complaints. The platform allows users to select their district, view MLA details, submit complaints related to local issues, and upload supporting evidence such as images and videos. Citizens can track the status of their complaints and view updates or responses from the concerned authorities. Government representatives can access submitted complaints, provide replies, update complaint statuses, and take necessary action to resolve issues. The main objective of MakkalConnect is to improve communication between the public and government, promote transparency and accountability, and make public services more accessible, efficient, and citizen-friendly.
